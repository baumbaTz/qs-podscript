Who transcribes

By default this server doesn't transcribe anything itself: “Transcribe again”, “Redo speaker detection” and new episodes wait for the helpers' computers – every connected QS-PodScript with “Transcribe for the server” ticked takes one episode at a time, so several computers work in parallel. The speaker detection settings above are sent along with every episode.

Moving to another computer

An export is one file with everything you did: podcasts, episodes, transcripts, corrections, people, voice samples, spelling fixes and the audio copies for listening. Speech models and tools are not included – the other computer downloads them during setup.

Import on this computer

Replaces all podcasts, transcripts and people here with the ones from the export. The data currently here is not deleted: it is moved to a folder before-import-… inside the data folder. This computer's graphics card and speech model settings stay as they are.

After the upload, close QS-PodScript and start it again – the data is swapped at the start.
